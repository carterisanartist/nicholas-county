# Nicholas County the Game — playtest builds

Walk, drive and brawl around the real Nicholas County, West Virginia. Start at home in Richwood;
the whole county is out there.

**Current build: 2026.11.14.0352** — The game is bigger than GitHub allows for one file, so it now downloads in parts. The launcher updates itself first, then fetches the parts, checks them and puts the game together; a download that stops picks up where it left off. If you download by hand from the Releases page, join the parts first (the download page says how). Missions: friends text you (the text lands in their thread on your phone) or wave you over, you talk, and a short mission starts. Press J, or open the menu, for the mission log: what's on, what you've finished, your best times and the texts that started them. Playing online, friends nearby join in and finish it with you. The Camry Run: Jacob bets you can't get from Starbound Skate Center in Craigsville to the Richwood GoMart in under twelve minutes. Line up on the grid, hold through the 3-2-1, and make every gate; bronze, silver and gold each pay once. Your best runs go on the open board (any car) and the Camry board (your white Camry), and up to three ghosts (yours and your friends') race along. Stuck or upside down for five seconds puts you back at the last gate with a five-second penalty. Parking lots open onto the streets beside them: no more curb wall trapping your car in a lot. Make your own person: at a new game, and any time at the mirror by Home's front door or the chair at Shear Mountain Salon, pick a body, height, weight and build, skin tone, a face (presets and sliders), hair style and color, facial hair, eye color, tattoos and scars, and a starting outfit. Friends online see the look you made. Cigarettes go to your lips instead of your neck, cans tip to your mouth, and you hold them at your side between drags and sips. Rifles are held with both hands, the butt in your shoulder and your cheek on the stock when you aim, and carried properly when you walk, for you, your friends and the NPC hunters. Rivers and creeks run for real: water flows downhill in carved channels with a current you can feel, riffles, rapids and rocks you can stand on. The Gauley's named rapids (Pillow Rock, Lost Paddle, Iron Ring, Sweet's Falls, Pure Screaming Hell and more) are where the river guides put them, and the big ones have holes that hold a swimmer before flushing them out. Swim anywhere there's water: wade in, tread water, swim against the current, dive under (Ctrl or C, B on a controller) and come back up (Space, A). Stamina and breath run down; black out under water and you come to on the nearest bank, hurt. Under water the view goes murky and sound is muffled. Jump in from any height: feet first or a dive goes in clean, a belly flop stings, and holding Shift on the way down tucks a cannonball. The game keeps your highest clean jump, the highlines you've crossed and the rapids you've swum. Summersville Lake's cliffs have jump spots over deep water (Long Point, Long Wall and more) and highlines rigged between cliff tops. Press E (Y) at an anchor to step on, balance with A and D or the stick, walk with W and S, and jump off with Space. Cars and water: a deep ford drowns out the engine (crank it again once it dries), water sucked into the intake locks it up, and deep or fast water floats a car off, carries it downstream and sinks it nose first. These are early playtest builds. The launcher installs the game, adds it to Steam, and pulls the newest build every
time you start it, so you only install once. Each start shows a small status window first: *Checking for updates…*,
then *Up to date: 2026.11.11.0532* (gone in a second or two), or the download with its progress and *Installed …*. With
no internet it says so and plays the build you have. The build before each update is kept, so if a new one misbehaves
the launcher menu can go back to it.

## Windows (desktop, laptop, ROG Ally)

1. Download **[Install-Nicholas-County.cmd](https://github.com/carterisanartist/nicholas-county/releases/latest/download/Install-Nicholas-County.cmd)** and double-click it.
   (If Windows SmartScreen warns about it: *More info → Run anyway*.)
2. The launcher opens and downloads the game (about 2.8 GB), then puts **Nicholas County** on your Desktop, in the Start
   menu, and in your Steam library.

Or from PowerShell:

```powershell
iex (New-Object Net.WebClient).DownloadString('https://github.com/carterisanartist/nicholas-county/releases/latest/download/install.ps1')
```

## Steam Deck (and Linux)

1. Switch to **Desktop Mode** (Steam button → Power → Switch to Desktop).
2. Open **Konsole** and run:

   ```bash
   curl -fsSL https://github.com/carterisanartist/nicholas-county/releases/latest/download/install.sh | bash
   ```

3. Switch back to Game Mode. **Nicholas County** is in your library under *Non-Steam*. Starting it updates it first
   (the status window shows over Game Mode). **Nicholas County Launcher** on the desktop has *Check for updates*, the
   installed and newest versions, and *Go back to the previous build*.

## Playing together

Needs Steam open and signed in. In the game press the **Menu** button (F1 on a keyboard) → **Online**. One person
hosts and reads out the 6-letter code (or invites Steam friends); everyone else joins. Works over the internet with no
port forwarding, up to 8 players. Steam shows you as playing "Spacewar" while online — that's normal for playtest
builds.

## Controls

Keyboard + mouse or any controller (Xbox, Steam Deck, ROG Ally); the on-screen prompts follow whatever you touched
last. **F** / **Y** gets in and out of cars, **M** / **View** opens the travel map, **F1** / **Menu** opens the menu.

## Manual downloads

Every build is on the [Releases](https://github.com/carterisanartist/nicholas-county/releases) page: the Windows zip and the Linux
tar.gz unpack to a `NicholasCounty` folder you can run directly (no auto-updates that way). A build over 1.5 GB comes in
parts (`.001`, `.002`, ...) because GitHub limits the size of each file; join them first:
`copy /b NicholasCounty-Windows-x64.zip.001 + NicholasCounty-Windows-x64.zip.002 NicholasCounty-Windows-x64.zip` in a
Command Prompt, or `cat NicholasCounty-Linux-x64.tar.gz.0* > NicholasCounty-Linux-x64.tar.gz` on Linux. `SHA256SUMS.txt`
next to them checks a download (parts and the joined file): `sha256sum -c --ignore-missing SHA256SUMS.txt` on Linux,
or compare with `Get-FileHash NicholasCounty-Windows-x64.zip` in PowerShell.
